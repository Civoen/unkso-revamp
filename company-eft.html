About the company

Delta Company runs planned raids as a disciplined squad. Routes, roles and extracts are agreed before deploy, and nobody gets left behind. Whether you’re new to Tarkov or a veteran, you’ll learn the maps and survive more raids with us.

Every Sunday

  1. −15 minForm upJoin Discord, check comms and get sorted into squads.
  2. StartBriefingYour squad leader sets the objective and gives everyone a role.
  3. FTXDeployRun the exercise as a unit, on the squad leader’s call.
  4. AfterAfter Action ReviewWhat went well, what to fix, and who stood out.

Example FTXs

A taste of the field training exercises the company runs on Sundays.

FTX screenshot
Close quarters

Operation Factory Floor

Short, intense raids on Factory to drill room clearing, communication and covering each other through tight corridors.

  • Agree an entry route and extract before deploy
  • Call every contact by direction and floor
  • Extract together, with no solo exits
FTX screenshot
Squad raid

Operation Customs Run

A planned raid across Customs with a set route, loot stops and a fallback extract if the plan goes wrong.

  • Follow the agreed route and checkpoints
  • Keep a rear guard watching the squad’s back
  • Reach the primary extract, or fall back to the secondary
FTX screenshot
Advanced

Operation Reserve Watch

A slower, high-risk raid on Reserve for experienced members, focused on overwatch, timing and extracting with the squad intact.

  • Set overwatch before the squad moves
  • Plan the extract timing before deploy
  • Bring every member home with their kit

All playstyles are welcome

You don’t need to be the best shot in the lobby. Every squad needs all three, and you’re free to try each one.

  • ShooterYou love the gunfight. You take the lead on contacts and win the engagements.
  • SupportYou keep the squad in the fight with medical aid, resupply, cover fire and overwatch.
  • ObjectiveYou play the mission. You capture, hold and extract while the squad covers you.
